# Tell me about a project that failed.

Difficulty: **HIGH** (estimated preparation effort).

## Question

Tell me about a project that failed.

## Input for your answer

Choose a real experience: the situation, your responsibility, constraints, decisions, and evidence of the outcome.

## Expected answer

Give a concise STAR response: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Explain your own contribution and what you learned. There is no single correct output.

The scenarios below are prompts to help you recall an experience, not claims about your history or model answers to memorize.

## Example scenario 1

A project launches but fails to achieve its intended adoption.

If you have a similar real example, explain your actions, why you chose them, what happened, and what you would change.

## Example scenario 2

A project is stopped after its central assumption is disproved.

If you have a similar real example, explain your actions, why you chose them, what happened, and what you would change.

Use the [answer framework](answer-framework.md) and [explanation](explanation.md) after selecting your example.
