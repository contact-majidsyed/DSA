# Describe a time you made a wrong technical decision.: Explanation

```text
Context -> Your responsibility -> Your actions -> Evidence of outcome
                                      |                   |
                                      v                   v
                               Why this choice?     What changed?
                                                          |
                                                          v
                                                    What learned?
```

Take ownership without inventing certainty in hindsight. Separate the original reasoning, the new evidence, and the corrective action.

Check that the listener can identify what you personally did, why you did it, and what happened. An honest unresolved result is better than an invented success. This is an answer framework, not a claim about your background.
