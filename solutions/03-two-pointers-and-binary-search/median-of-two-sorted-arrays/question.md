# Median of Two Sorted Arrays

Difficulty: **HIGH**

## Question

Return the median of two ascending arrays. At least one array must be nonempty.

## Input

Two ascending integer arrays a and b; at least one is nonempty.

## Output

The median of all values combined, as a floating-point number.

## Example 1

```text
Input: a = [1,3], b = [2]
Output: 2.0
```

The combined sorted values are [1,2,3].

## Example 2

```text
Input: a = [1,2], b = [3,4]
Output: 2.5
```

Average the middle values 2 and 3.
