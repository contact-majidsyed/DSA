# Container With Most Water

Difficulty: **MEDIUM**

## Question

Return maximum water area between two nonnegative heights.

## Input

An array height of nonnegative vertical-line heights; indices give horizontal positions.

## Output

The largest area between two lines and the horizontal axis.

## Example 1

```text
Input: height = [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7]
Output: 49
```

Indices 1 and 8 give width 7 and limiting height 7.

## Example 2

```text
Input: height = [1,1]
Output: 1
```

Width 1 times height 1 equals 1.
